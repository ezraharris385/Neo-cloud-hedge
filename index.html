DSPterm-sheet ready

A hyperscaler or operator fund writes the landlord a put: if the shell goes dark they step in at 85% of rent within 90 days. Caps downtime and floors the re-let spread.

NDS-Bcall your lawyers

A digital CDS written by AI-bull funds: pays the full notional on the default event, whatever the actual loss. Overshoots small losses, undershoots big ones — priced at a 2.2× load.

AIW-CATnever been done

An SPV issues notes whose principal is wiped on a dual trigger: this tenant defaults and the market is in correction. Investors clip a fat coupon for holding the AI-winter tail.

GRIDneeds a consortium

NeoCloud tenants mutually insure each other’s leases, FDIC-style. Honest catch: in a correction the pool drains — modeled at an 80% honor rate exactly when you need it.

RVGstreet-legal

The OEM guarantees a resale floor on the fleet — lifts the lien’s fire-sale haircut from 45% to 62% and removes the correction discount. Needs the GPU lien switched on.

WARstreet-legal

Warrants over tenant equity. Worth nothing in the default paths, so the tail doesn’t move — this one pays you for bearing the risk instead of reducing it. Shows up as income.

    Model assumptions & method
    

      
Hazard. Base annual default hazard by stage (Seed 24% → growth 6%), scaled by runway (<9 mo ×1.6 … >24 mo ×0.85), contracted-backlog coverage weighted by counterparty grade, and top-customer concentration (>50% ×1.15, >70% ×1.3). A seasoning shape peaks in year 2 and decays — survivors mature.

      
Regimes. Each path draws a persistent AI-market regime: boom / base / correction. Hazard multiplier 0.55 / 1.0 / 1.9, re-let downtime 4 / 9 / 14 mo, re-let rent spread +15% / 0 / −22%, GPU resale ×1.15 / ×1.0 / ×0.6. The outlook selector tilts regime probabilities.

      
Loss given default. Rent arrears (2–5 mo) + vacancy downtime + PV of the re-let rent spread over the remaining term (8% discount) + re-leasing costs (commissions plus a 3%-of-capex fit-out refresh). Losses are capped at remaining lease value; boom paths can produce re-let gains — scarce powered shell is genuinely valuable.

      
Support stack. Applied in seniority order: deposit → LC (ratable burn-down) → GPU lien (30%/yr depreciation, 45% fire-sale haircut, second lien recovers 25%) → revenue assignment (counterparty survival × 35% collection realization) → sponsor guarantee (90% honor rate) → insurance (excess of all collateral, premium = modeled layer loss × 1.75).

      
Exotic desk. Compute-indexed rent scales the correction-regime hazard down with participation and softens the re-let spread (the lease revenue covaries with the tenant’s). The dark-shell put caps downtime at 3 months, floors the spread at −15% and caps re-leasing costs. The binary swap pays its full notional on any default event (2.2× load); the cat bond pays actual loss up to principal only in correction-regime defaults (1.35× load + issuance); the mutual pool honors at 80% in corrections, 95% otherwise; the residual-value guarantee lifts the GPU haircut to 62% and removes the correction discount; warrants add expected income without touching the tail.

      
Metrics. Expected loss and CVaR₉₅ (mean of the worst 5% of paths) on 6,000 seeded paths; identical draws across configurations, so comparisons are noise-free. The solver greedily adds the increment with the best tail-risk reduction per dollar of annual cost until CVaR₉₅ clears tolerance.
